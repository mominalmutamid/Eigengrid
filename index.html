Examples and syntax tips
Powers: x^2. Multiply: 2*x or 2x. Constants: pi, e. Functions: sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, sqrt, abs, log (natural), log10, exp. A new letter such as a in a*sin(x) makes a slider. Polar: r=2*sin(3*t). Parametric: (cos(t), sin(t)); t=0..6.28 Inequalities: y>x^2 or x^2+y^2<=25. Piecewise: y={x<0: -x, x>=0: x}. Slope field: y'=x-y. Animate by using the word time. Tap a curve to trace it. Drag to move, pinch to zoom.
